Peck runs on your computer. These are the only services DEVLIN.AI LLC uses to provide Peck and to answer you:

Subprocessor What for Data it handles Location

Services you choose to connect (your AI provider, your GitHub account, Vercel, Supabase) are yours, not our subprocessors: Peck connects to them for you, under your accounts. The same goes for Context7, the documentation service Peck's default settings give your agents: your agent talks to it directly, it handles no data on our behalf, and you can remove it in Settings.

We update this list when we add or change a subprocessor, and note the change in Peck's release notes. Questions: legal@devlin.ai.
